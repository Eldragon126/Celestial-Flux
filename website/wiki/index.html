CORE IDENTITY

A physics collapse simulator where survival is gravity manipulation.

What It Is

Vector Anomaly is a run-based physics roguelike arena survival game. The player survives by reading gravity fields, preserving momentum, slingshotting around mass, and turning dangerous physics into recovery routes.

What It Is Not

It should not be framed as a generic twin-stick shooter, loot fantasy, bullet spam game, or power fantasy. The public hook is watchable impossibility that becomes understandable after survival.

Familiar Shapewaves, builds, boss anchors, retries
Unfamiliar Substancegravity, time, resonance, rupture, law mutation
Failure Rulefast to understand, fair in retrospect

PLAYER SYSTEMS

Movement is the primary survival skill.

Thrust

Thrust accelerates along the ship vector and spends energy. Good thrust decisions preserve route options instead of simply pointing away from danger.

Precision Drag

Drag ON is the control style: tactical braking, tangent cleanup, recovery routing, and small energy returns inside gravity windows.

Momentum Mode

Drag OFF preserves high-speed motion. It is riskier, clip-friendlier, and better for players who can read a future path under pressure.

Trajectory Predictor

The orbital trajectory predictor is production-facing, not debug clutter. It should stay bright and readable: orange immediate danger, cyan continuation, glow, fade, and compact origin marker.

RESONANCE FIELDS

Overlapping gravity becomes tactical field language.

PULL IN

Compression

Bodies fall toward the zone core. Use it to bunch enemies, bend shots, or dive through danger.

PUSH OUT

Inversion

Bodies are pushed outward. Use it for emergency escape routes and projectile denial.

FLOW ARC

Slipstream

Bodies slide along the tangent. Use it to turn panic into lateral speed.

SLOW SHOTS

Temporal Scar

Enemies and hostile projectiles lose time locally while the player stays responsive.

ORBIT BEND

Harmonic Orbit

Bodies curve into stable arcs for trick shots, recovery loops, and apex payoffs.

Every field should answer: pull, push, flow, slow, or bend.

Rule Of Thumb

If the screen becomes noisy, look for the verb first. Pull moves inward, push moves outward, flow moves sideways, slow reduces threat timing, and orbit bend curves paths.

FIELD READING

The gravity heat map makes invisible force tactical.

How To Toggle

Press F10 on keyboard or D-pad up on controller. The overlay draws relative potential contours, gradient vectors, and a golden tangent guide near the most useful gravity source.

Performance Contract

The heat map uses an adaptive capped grid, limited source buffers, packed sample arrays, and gradient stride so it can remain a tactical overlay instead of a full simulation tax.

CAMPAIGN MODE

Defend the home planet while the map becomes a trade route.

Mothership Docking

Friendly and trader motherships broadcast dock prompts when the player is close. Confirming dock opens the dedicated dock scene and holds the live campaign until undock.

Energy-Credit Trade

Credits buy escorts, repair and armor, speed, damage, slingshot upgrades, and hijack beacons. The trade readout shows current credits and formation state.

Hostile Carriers

Hostile motherships refuse docking, fire on the player, award credits when destroyed, and give the campaign map more than one decision point between waves. Friendly carriers also help shoot raiders.

CO-OP FOUNDATION

LAN co-op exists behind a single session contract.

Current Flow

One player hosts and plays. Other players join by IP and port. The host owns run start, restart, scene flow, and deterministic run config.

Co-op Payoff

Distinct player vector events can combine into shared resonance/time payoffs through CoopComboDirector without changing solo mechanics.

NET7 Readiness

Sessions now surface protocol, mod signature, platform, average ping, quality label, and client run-ready acknowledgements in status snapshots.

Sync Boundaries

Player network state includes input-device and slingshot metadata, while hot-path sync queries use capped RuntimeRegistry buffers.

Steam Roadmap

Steam support should be a transport adapter behind NetworkSession, not a gameplay fork.
