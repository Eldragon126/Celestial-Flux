# ORBITRON: VECTORFALL Universe Guide

This guide gives the mechanics a shared fiction without turning the game into lore-first exposition. The universe exists to make gravity mastery, collapsing laws, and survival under impossible momentum feel coherent.

## Core Fiction

ORBITRON is a failing vector simulation. The player is not conquering space; they are surviving inside a physical model that is losing stability while still obeying readable rules.

Every run is a reconstruction from a seed. The arena is rebuilt from stable anchors, then allowed to collapse again through gravity, resonance, time dilation, and law mutation.

## Player Role

The player is the remaining control vector: a small ship or probe capable of converting acceleration, orbit angle, and gravity exposure into survival. The player does not dominate the simulation. They learn to ride it.

## System Terms

- `Vectorfall`: the point where stable movement becomes impossible unless the player uses gravity correctly.
- `Arena Law`: the seeded physical rule profile shaping a run.
- `Resonance`: overlapping gravity information becoming a tactical field rule.
- `Temporal Scar`: a local time wound where enemies and projectiles lose time.
- `Gravity Scar`: a memory left in the arena after extreme motion or collapse.
- `Rupture`: the endgame state where the wave generator fails and laws recombine.
- `Resonance Singularity`: the final music-synchronized failure point of the simulation.

## Progression As Story

Early game:

- The simulation is sterile and readable.
- The player learns clean vector movement and basic gravity pressure.

Mid game:

- Arena laws become more visible.
- Resonance, time pockets, and rare events reveal that the arena is not static.

Late game:

- Bosses stop feeling like enemies with patterns and start feeling like physics rules with bodies.
- Impossible events appear, but remain deterministic and capped.

Rupture:

- The wave system goes offline.
- Familiar rules recombine under pressure.

Music finale:

- Sound becomes the timing authority for reality.
- The final boss is not just hostile; it is the simulation trying to synchronize itself.

Credits:

- The simulation becomes inert.
- Pressure is removed so the player gets emotional decompression instead of another challenge loop.

## Writing Rules

Use language that sounds like:

- simulation telemetry
- physics diagnosis
- survival readout
- controlled failure

Avoid language that sounds like:

- military command
- loot fantasy
- generic space adventure
- random chaos

## Example In-Game Copy

- `LAWS CRACKING: WAVE GENERATOR OFFLINE`
- `ARENA LAW: MIRROR WELL`
- `PHYSICS MUTATION DETECTED`
- `DEATH VECTOR: low momentum left you pinned`
- `MUSIC DEFINES REALITY`

The text should make the player feel that the arena is understandable, even when it is terrifying.
