
ORBITAL DRIFT (CELESTIAL FLUX) - CONTRIBUTOR MODULAR IDEAS

Goal: Only ADD new nodes or scripts. Avoid modifying core scripts (base_enemy.gd, player.gd, etc.) to prevent breaking the game. Use groups like "planets" and "Player" and standard methods like take_damage().

These are just ideas. If you are interested, write your name next to them so that other people don't repeat what you are doing.

If you have your own idea, feel free to run it by me (see the GitHub README) and do that.

--- 👾 NEW ENEMY VARIANTS ---
[Standard: Use base_enemy.gd logic for gravity and movement]

1. The "Leech" Parasite:
   - Logic: Instead of bouncing back on contact (_on_attack_area_body_entered), it reparents itself to the Player or uses a RemoteTransform2D to "stick."
   - Action: Calls body.take_damage() every 1.5 seconds.
   - Requirement: Must be destroyable while attached to free the player.

2. The "Splitting" Asteroid-Bot:
   - Logic: Listen for the _on_health_component_died() signal.
   - Action: Before queue_free(), instantiates 2-3 smaller, faster versions of itself at its current global_position.

3. The "Shielder" Support:
   - Logic: Uses the player_shield.gd hexagonal logic.
   - Action: Orbits a "base_shooter_enemy" and protects it. Its shield must be destroyed before the main enemy can take damage.

4. The Gravity Harasser:
   - Logic: Has a 'mass' variable similar to black_hole.gd or planet_1.gd.
   - Action: Doesn't shoot; simply follows the player to disrupt their flight path with its own localized gravity field.

5. Sniper Turret:
   - Logic: Static or slow-moving.
   - Action: Fires high-velocity projectiles only when the player is within a specific long-range radius.

--- 🌌 CELESTIAL HAZARDS (Add to "planets" group) ---

   - Logic: Inherit from planet_1.gd.

2. Nebula Clouds (Area2D):

3. Wormholes:
   - Logic: Create a pair of nodes.
   - Action: On body_entered, set body.global_position to the exit node's position.
